## 설명 요약

SAVLoc: Structure-Aided Visual Localization for PX4 in Warehouses - Michael Baldwin, Self-Employed This talk will present the development and integration of a novel vision-based localization method known as “SAVLoc”, or Structure-Aided Visual Localization, to a PX4 drone for autonomous navigation in warehouse environments. The pipeline utilized a unique approach leveraging a custom-trained YoloV8 network for reliably detecting and segmenting the racking-structures comprising warehouse shelving.
