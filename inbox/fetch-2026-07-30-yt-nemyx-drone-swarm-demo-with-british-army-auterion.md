## 설명 요약

Swarming at scale no longer requires one operator per drone. Nemyx is Auterion’s software layer for one-to-many swarm command. Last week in the UK, we demonstrated Nemyx to the British Army. Commanded by a single operator, the demonstration showed rapid deployment, coordinated launch, formation movement, and precision strike. Nemyx delivers autonomous mass through open, software-defined autonomy.
