## Abstract

This study aimed to determine optimal conditions for Watershed algorithm-based individual tree crown segmentation using UAV-derived Canopy Height Model (CHM) and grayscale imagery. Three sample plots were established in coniferous forests (Pinus koraiensis and Larix kaempferi) and a broadleaf forest (Quercus mongolica) in Pocheon-si, Gyeonggi-do, and Cheongju-si, Chungcheongbuk-do, South Korea. CHM and grayscale images were constructed at spatial resolutions of 5 cm, 25 cm, and 50 cm. Gap regions were classified using three thresholding methods (Otsu, Entropy, and Percentile 5%, 10%, 15%), followed by individual tree crown segmentation using the Watershed algorithm.
