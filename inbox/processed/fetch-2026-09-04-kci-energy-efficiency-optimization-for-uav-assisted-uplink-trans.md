# Energy efficiency optimization for UAV-assisted uplink transmissions over probabilistic LoS channels

**Authors**: Guo Lili(School of Information Science and Technology, Nantong University, Nantong, China)
**Journal**: ICT Express

## Abstract

This article studies a ground-to-air uplink transmission, where a fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) is deployed to gather data from multiple ground users (GUs) over probabilistic line-of-sight channels. With the aim of achieving the maximal system energy efficiency, a joint optimization problem consisting of three variable blocks, i.e., time-slot scheduling, GUs’ transmit power control and the UAV’s trajectory design are studied. By utilizing the block coordinate descent technique, a joint optimization algorithm addressing the three variable blocks jointly is developed. Simulation results confirmed the effectiveness and efficiency of the proposed algorithm.
