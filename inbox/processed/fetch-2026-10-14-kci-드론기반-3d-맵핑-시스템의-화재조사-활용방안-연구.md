## Abstract

Rapid urbanization and industrialization have increased the complexity of fire scenes, revealing the limitations of traditional investigation methods. Conventional approaches often expose personnel to life-threatening hazards, such as toxic gases and structural collapses, while relying on subjective 2D photography and manual measurements. To address these challenges, this study develops a methodology utilizing drone-based 3D mapping and AI analysis to enhance the speed, accuracy, and safety of fire investigations. By integrating high-resolution thermal imaging and 3D spatial data, the proposed system allows for the digital reconstruction of complex scenes without requiring immediate human entry into dangerous areas.
