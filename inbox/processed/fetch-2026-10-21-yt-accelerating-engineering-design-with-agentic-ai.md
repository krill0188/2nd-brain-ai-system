## 자막 (자동 추출, 최대 8000자)

Generative AI can create code and tests quickly, so you can spend more time deciding what to implement and how. But as an engineer, you still need to prove it meets requirements. So how do you build the engineering evidence needed to confidently use AI-generated output in your design? You bring domain knowledge, your specific requirements, and how you want to address them. You also provide design intent and context to your AI agent. The agent uses its reasoning to draft a plan, but it needs a way to execute those steps, and it needs to know how. That's where MATLAB and Simulink do what they do best, modeling the math and science, creating and managing tests, simulating and debugging, and providing the visualization and evidence so you can understand, trust, and own the results. We want to use the strengths of each. AI excels at general-purpose reasoning, but it needs tools to model the math and physics. MATLAB and Simulink supply that ground truth. AI can generate designs or code that include details not explicitly specified. Consequently, understanding and verification become the bottlenecks. MATLAB and Simulink deterministically execute, validate, and visualize AI outputs. You can review the results, evaluate alternatives, and decide what belongs in the design. MATLAB and Simulink still provide the math and science algorithms to model and verify your designs. AI automates the supporting work, and you remain in charge. Let's see how this team works together to fix a bug in an automotive subsystem. Here, the yaw rate calculator and side slip angle estimator models process the physical sensor data, but there's a bug in the logic that uses this output to control the brakes. You provide your knowledge of the problem. The AI agent uses tools to analyze the design, isolate the bug, generate a test case, and propose a fix. You still own the design, so you need the visibility and understanding to evaluate the proposed fix. From there, the AI agent implements your fix, documents it, and updates your test suite. Your engineering rigor doesn't change, but you can apply it to more designs, more tests, and more iterations. MATLAB and Simulink, together with Agentry AI, help you solve more engineering problems while building the evidence needed to show your design meets requirements.
