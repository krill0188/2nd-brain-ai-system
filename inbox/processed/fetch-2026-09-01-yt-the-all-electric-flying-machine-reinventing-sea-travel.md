Meet REGENT Craft, a Rhode Island-based startup reinventing sea travel with the Viceroy Seaglider, an all-electric maritime vehicle that flies just above the water's surface. Designed to shorten coastal commutes while reducing carbon emissions, the Seaglider operates in three modes: hull mode like a traditional boat, hydrofoil mode where advanced foils lift the craft above the water to reduce drag, and flight mode where it harnesses ground effect to reach top speed.
