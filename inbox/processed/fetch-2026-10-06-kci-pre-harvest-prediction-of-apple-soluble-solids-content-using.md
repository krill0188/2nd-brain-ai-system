## Abstract

This study developed and validated machine learning models for predicting the pre-harvest soluble solids content (SSC) of apples using only vegetation indices derived from UAV-based multispectral imagery. A total of 39 'Fuji' apple trees across 10 orchards in Andong, Gyeongsangbuk-do, Republic of Korea, were surveyed over two growing seasons (2024– 2025). Imagery was acquired at noon on 17 September 2024 and 16 September 2025 using a Trinity F90+ fixed-wing UAV equipped with an Altum-PT multispectral sensor, flown at an altitude of 150 m with 75% overlap, yielding a ground sample distance (GSD) of 3.17 cm/pixel.
