## 설명 요약

Explore how phased arrays enable integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) systems to communicate with and localize users by using shared spectrum, waveforms, and hardware. This communication-centric ISAC workflow in MATLAB® models a MIMO-OFDM link with a phased-array transmitter and receiver in a multipath environment.
