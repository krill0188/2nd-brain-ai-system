## Abstract

Advances in autonomous flight, intelligent sensing, and edge computing have enabled unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to demonstrate significant potential in low-altitude logistics, campus delivery, and emergency supply transportation. This paper presents the design and implementation of an autonomous delivery UAV system built on ROS2. This system is capable of autonomous task execution, including waypoint navigation, arrival at a target area, visual detection of landing markers, and precise landing. For reliable visual perception during landing, a deep learning-based object detection model is deployed onboard to identify preset landing markers from real-time camera feeds.
