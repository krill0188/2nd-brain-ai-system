## 설명 요약

Competing drone companies just proved they can solve interoperability faster in the open than any of them can alone. Watch highlights from the Dronecode Foundation's MAVLink-Military hackathon, where 14 U.S. and international companies integrated one open standard across flight controllers, payloads, and ground control stations in just 48 hours, then flew working demos for government and industry guests in Manassas, Virginia.
