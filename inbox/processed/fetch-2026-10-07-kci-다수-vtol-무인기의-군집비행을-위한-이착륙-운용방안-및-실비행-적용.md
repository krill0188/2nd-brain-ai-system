## Abstract

This study proposes an operational approach to takeoff and landing for swarm flight of multiple VTOL fixed-wing UAVs and evaluates its applicability through outdoor flight tests. The takeoff operation consists of simultaneous vertical takeoff of all UAVs, altitude separation based on group-specific designated altitudes, and sequential group departures, with the UAVs in each departing group performing transition in parallel before entering swarm flight. The landing operation consists of spatial separation, sequential swarm exit, reverse transition, and landing. In a flight test with 20 UAVs divided into two groups, all UAVs completed transition within 20.5s from the initiation of transition by the leading group after reaching their designated altitudes.
