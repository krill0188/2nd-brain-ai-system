## 설명 요약

Now, you can use Emlid Corrections with Reach RX or Reach RX2 directly in PIX4Dcatch for accurately geotagged 3D scans: no need to configure a separate RTK network and manually enter addresses, ports, credentials, or mountpoints. Once you activate Emlid Corrections, PIX4Dcatch receives centimeter-accurate positioning from Reach while you scan, giving you accurately geotagged 3D scans ready for processing in PIX4Dcloud or PIX4Dmatic. Learn more about Emlid Corrections: https://emlid.com/rtk-corre
