Why combine MuJoCo and ROS 2? I'll explain it easily in one minute. This robot isn't real. It's a virtual robot inside a computer. There are two main types of robot practice programs. Gazebo is a fully-equipped camper, and MuJoCo is a light, fast tent. MuJoCo is so lightweight it runs on an ordinary laptop. In games, things shake when you grab them, right? MuJoCo is stable because its calculations are precise the moment it grabs something. Plug in a USB-C cable for the robot, and you can use your existing smart tools right away.
