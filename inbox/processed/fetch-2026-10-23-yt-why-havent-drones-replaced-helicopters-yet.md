Why aren't drones being used more to replace helicopters? Well, in many cases, we already do. Drones have taken over jobs like aerial photography, inspections, and surveying. But carrying people is where it gets harder. Batteries hold far less energy than jet fuel, so flight time is shorter. And on many multi-rotors, losing a rotor can be serious, while a helicopter can auto rotate down. And there's also a ton of testing that has to happen before these aircraft can be considered reliable and safe. Some companies are even developing passenger drones like the Jetson one. But for now, helicopters still have specific use cases that drones haven't quite been able to replace yet. Let us know in the comments if you think drones will replace helicopters anytime soon.
