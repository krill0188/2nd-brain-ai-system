# 신경망 기반 센서고장 보상과 IMU 기반 구동기고장 보상을 통합한 쿼드로터 UAV의 복합 고장 보완 기법

## Abstract

This paper proposes an integrated fault estimation and accommodation scheme for quadrotor UAVs by using neural-network-based sensor fault compensation and actuator fault compensation. The proposed method directly applies online fault estimates to the compensation loop without a separate fault decision stage. The sizes of sensor faults are estimated using a neural-network observer and compensated through measurement correction, while actuator faults are estimated from IMU-based thrust and torque calculations and reflected in control input reconfiguration. Simulation results show that the proposed method effectively estimates fault magnitudes and maintains stable flights under both single and complex faults of sensors and actuators.
