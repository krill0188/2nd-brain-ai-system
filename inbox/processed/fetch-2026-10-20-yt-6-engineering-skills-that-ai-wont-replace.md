## 설명 요약

AI tools can generate engineering artifacts faster than ever, but fast implementation does not guarantee that you are solving the right problem or producing a trustworthy result. Starting with an AI-generated inverted pendulum model in Simscape™ and a controller model in Simulink®, you will explore why engineering judgment remains essential even when answers are inexpensive and implementation is nearly instant. Learn six durable skills that can help you direct AI tools and evaluate their output.
