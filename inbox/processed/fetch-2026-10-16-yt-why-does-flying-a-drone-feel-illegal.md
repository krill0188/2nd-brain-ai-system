## 자막 (자동 추출, 최대 8000자)

When you fly a drone, do you ever feel like you're breaking the law? Well, that's completely normal. Between the endless rules to keep up with, bystanders who only know drones from surveillance or war headlines, and no drone zone signs at every good spot, it's easy to feel like a criminal. The ironic part is I still feel it. And I work in the drone world, research drone rules for hours, teach people to fly, and have flown drones for 6 years. My best tip is to be confident in what you know. If someone walks up and asks what you're doing, you can stay calm and answer clearly and explain the rules and how you're following them if need be. And a lot of times, people will be very surprised and curious to learn more. Knowing the rules also shows you exactly where you can fly and how to get permission to fly in somewhat difficult areas. It can be frustrating, but it's worth it, and it's a good reminder of why most of us do this. Drones are just little flying cameras that let you see the world from a whole new perspective, and they can be a lot of fun.
