## Abstract

This paper presents an unmanned aerial vehicle mobile edge computing (UAV-MEC) framework in which multiple UAVs autonomously determine task offloading and computational resource allocation through a multi-agent system (MAS) and an Integrated Particle-Ant Algorithm (IPAA) for a single cell environment. The hybrid optimization methodology of Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) for each global exploration and refined searching in later stages that is to improve convergence quality and robustness.
