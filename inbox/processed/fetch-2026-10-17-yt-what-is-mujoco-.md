## 자막 (자동 추출, 최대 8000자)

Have you heard of MuJoCo? How do robots learn to walk? If they practice with a real robot, it breaks every time it falls. So, they practice in a computer simulation first. That is what MuJoCo is. In the computer, it’s okay if it falls thousands of times. You can even fast-forward time. Then, you transfer what it learned to the real robot. Google DeepMind made it free for anyone to use. So why did the Pink Lab workshop connect MuJoCo and ROS 2? I'll tell you in the next video.
