## 설명 요약

Tired of having your web scraper get blocked after only a few requests? In this tutorial, we build a Node.js scraper to track multi-region storefront prices and look at why standard scrapers run directly into strict rate limits. You'll learn how adding a simple three-line residential proxy configuration routes requests through localized, rotating IPs to bypass blocks completely.
