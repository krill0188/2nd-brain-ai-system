## 설명 요약

Aerospace Toolbox provides standards-based tools for designing aerospace vehicles and analyzing their motion, mission, and environment. You can interpret flight and mission data by using aerospace math, coordinate-frame transformations, and environment functions. You can observe vehicle motion and missions using 2D and 3D viewers, along with standard cockpit instruments. ## 자막 (자동 추출, 최대 8000자)

Design, analyze, and visualize space missions, aircraft dynamics, and flight data with Aerospace Toolbox. Use validated functions for coordinate transformations, environment models, unit conversions, quaternion math, and gas dynamics as a foundation for aerospace workflows. Leveraging this foundation, you can evaluate how aerospace vehicles perform throughout missions in their operating environments. For space mission analysis, you can define scenarios with satellites, ground stations, and other platforms. Propagate orbits, design constellations, model trajectories and maneuvers, analyze line-of-site access, and identify eclipse intervals for missions around Earth, the Moon, and other celestial bodies. For aircraft analysis, you can model aircraft configurations using imported aerodynamic data to support stability analysis, flying qualities assessment, and control design workflows. And throughout the workflow, visualization helps turn analysis results into engineering insight. Use 2D and 3D viewers, mission displays, vehicle motion visualization, and standard aerospace instruments to understand and communicate vehicle and mission behavior. With Aerospace Toolbox, you can analyze and design space missions, aircraft dynamics, and flight data for clearer insights into the vehicle motion, mission geometry, and system behavior. The toolbox supports agentic AI workflows with aerospace skills available in the MATLAB agentic toolkit.
