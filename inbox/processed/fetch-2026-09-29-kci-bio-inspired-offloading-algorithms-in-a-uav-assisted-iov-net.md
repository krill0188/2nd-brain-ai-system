## Abstract

This paper presents performance evaluation of various computation offloading algorithms in a UAV (Unmanned Aerial Vehicle)-assisted Internet of Vehicles (IoV) network with Mobile Edge Computing (MEC) for various heterogeneous vehicle traffic conditions. Three representative road topologies — signalized intersections (T1), multi-lane highways (T2), and mixed urban corridors (T3) — are modeled to capture the spatially and temporally varying computational demands inherent in real-world vehicular environments. A directional lane-aware congestion index ρ(t) is introduced to extend conventional density-based state modeling by incorporating signal phase timing and lane-level vehicle density.
