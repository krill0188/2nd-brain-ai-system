How is ESPN's game drone footage legal? A fan watching some college football games saw drone shots over tens of thousands of people and wondered if every fan had to give their approval. Let's break it down. Under Part 107, flying over people is allowed when either the drone meets certain categories or you get a waiver. If I had to guess, I'm assuming the production team got a waiver for flying over people as that would be the simplest option. Plus, they would need special FAA authorization to fly inside the stadium restriction, which usually runs from an hour before the game to an hour after.
