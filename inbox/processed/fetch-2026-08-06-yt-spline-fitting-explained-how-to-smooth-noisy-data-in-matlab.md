## 설명 요약

Spline fitting is an alternative to polynomial fitting for engineering and scientific data. When data is noisy, irregular, or densely sampled, fitting a single high-degree polynomial often leads to overfitting and wild oscillations between data points. Splines solve this by breaking the data into segments and fitting smooth, low-degree piecewise polynomials to each one.
