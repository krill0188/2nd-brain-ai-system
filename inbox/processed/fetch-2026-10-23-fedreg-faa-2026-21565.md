# Establishment of Class E Airspace; Havana, IL

**문서 유형**: Rule

## 요약(Abstract)

This action establishes Class E airspace at Mason District Hospital Heliport, Havana, IL. This action supports new instrument procedures and instrument flight rule (IFR) operations.
