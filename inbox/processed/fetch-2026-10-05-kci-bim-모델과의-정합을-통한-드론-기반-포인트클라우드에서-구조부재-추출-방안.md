## Abstract

Point Cloud-based 3D data are increasingly used for construction management. However, previous structural element extraction studies have mainly relied on terrestrial LiDAR. Drone photogrammetry enables rapid and non-contact data acquisition over large areas, but the resulting Point Clouds can be affected by occlusion, shooting direction, temporary structures, stored materials, and adjacent structural members. In this study, a Point Cloud generated from an actual reinforced concrete construction site was registered with an IFC-based BIM model. Columns, beams, walls, and slabs were extracted using point-to-mesh distance filtering and elementspecific rule-based processing.
